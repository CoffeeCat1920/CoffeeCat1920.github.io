I believe a desktop environment should stay completely out of your way. Every pixel has a purpose, every window snaps into place predictably, and your fingers rarely leave the home row. My workstation is built on Arch Linux with Sway (Wayland), styled in the warm, eye-friendly tones of Gruvbox Dark.

"Blazingly fast isn't just about startup benchmarks; it's about eliminating friction between a thought and its execution."
